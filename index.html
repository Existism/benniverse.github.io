What is a Ben?
A Ben is a person or thing that has been named Ben. The name Ben carries with it interesting side effects. Many Bens possess otherworldly powers and interesting fates. By being named Ben, a being is elevated to having an important role in the timeline of the universe. Despite not all Bens having powers or being strong, by having the name Ben, you are entered into the wider storylines of the Benniverse. For entities that are more abstract (ex. Sunny Ben), being dubbed with the title of Ben qualifies you to become a Ben and therefore enter into the running to acquire powers and play a role in the world as a whole. Now, not everything can become a Ben. Most Bens are created at birth and are usually human, while a lucky few are not human. The criteria to become a Ben are to have the name of Ben at birth; it cannot be acquired after being born. Once most Bens reach an age threshold, they stop aging. Although some Bens do continue to age, the threshold is different for each Ben.
